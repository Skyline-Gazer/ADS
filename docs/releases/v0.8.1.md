# ADS v0.8.1 — Refine, Don't Reinvent

**Status:** **published** — annotated tag `v0.8.1`, merged to `main` through Release PR [#148](https://github.com/Skyline-Gazer/ADS/pull/148). The previous baseline, [v0.8.0 Personal Engineering Glue](https://github.com/Skyline-Gazer/ADS/releases/tag/v0.8.0), remains immutable at `178212c41cce8fbf9354d4066e0f77341f6f23c4`. This is the final framework release: ADS framework development ends here.

**Theme:** *Refine, Don't Reinvent* — the last substantive scope increment of ADS v0.8.x. No new runtime, CLI, registry, schema, profile, gate or mandatory tooling. Every change is a correction or a minimal interpretation of existing principles, derived from recorded consumer evidence and validated against the published v0.8.0 text.

## Not included (unchanged dispositions)

No model router/scheduler/controller, no ADS lockfile schema or registry, no new mandatory profile or CI gate, no requirement-provenance registry, no governance-debt score, no mandatory per-PR Learning Loop or adoption certification, no consumer pin changes. Track G (Learning) remains NO_CHANGE: all recorded triggers map onto existing QUALITY anchors.

## Verification

- `python3 scripts/check-ads.py` PASS; `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests` — **221 tests**, all passing (197 pre-existing + 24 new doc-contract cases).
- Exact-head CI on the candidate PR: **4/4 success** (`validate` push + PR, `Isolated consumer / KIRARI`, `Isolated consumer / AiringCal`).
- Independent reviews (separate host sessions, not the authoring context), of unequal scope: a **completed full review** of `83ca9a6..06e4409` returned `APPROVE_WITH_NITS` (6 nits, all remediated in `de000d3`); the attempted **full delta review** of `06e4409..1c62eb5` did not complete (model-route timeouts twice, `NOT_VERIFIED`); one **scoped** review of the new SAFETY paragraph returned `APPROVE` with one precision nit (applied in `df61fed`). The test-only commit `d4d64d7` has suite/CI evidence but no separate review. Part of the post-`06e4409` delta therefore received no second-session review.
- AI-native behavioral validation on real `omp/19.1.1`, isolated temporary repositories, short owner prompts, v0.8.0 baseline vs candidate under matched host/model conditions. Scenarios S1–S3 (unsupported-requirement judgment, autonomous delivery, scope correction) produced comparable decisions, with candidate runs slower and three wrap-ups truncated by the time cap. **S4 (legacy strict → Lite migration) is `PARTIAL`:** the in-place arm produced a verified commit; the clean re-adoption arm did not finish inside its cap, so the two migration routes were **not** shown to reach an equivalent outcome. All runs were single-shot on one model route with no cross-host comparison and no efficiency metric; a correct pin, green CI or a limited experiment is not evidence that engineering outcomes improved. Results and limits are recorded in #143.

## Compatibility, migration, rollback

- Standalone consumers: unaffected unless they choose to re-copy the template; new sentences are additive interpretations.
- Pinned consumers: existing pins keep working byte-identically; the update guidance now states explicitly that a pin refresh is not a policy migration (and vice versa).
- Rollback: revert the relevant doc commit(s); consumers restore their actual previously verified ADS pin. No schema, state or migration code exists.
- Historical refs (`v0.2`–`v0.8.0`, `archive/cnb-dev-final`) remain immutable and untouched.

## Freeze

ADS framework development ends at v0.8.1. `STANDARD.md`, `SAFETY.md`, `QUALITY.md`, the Skills, the optional profiles, the governance scripts and the consumer adoption framework stay published as reference material and are not extended further. Ongoing maintenance moves to [`personal/AGENTS.md`](../../personal/AGENTS.md), changed through ordinary Git commits rather than new releases. That file, delivered by [PR #149](https://github.com/Skyline-Gazer/ADS/pull/149), is present in the tagged tree but is outside this release's normative scope.
